Indian Pilgrims Killed in Nepal Bus Crash

KATHMANDU, March 15, 2026 —

Seven Indian pilgrims were killed and nine others injured when their bus left a mountain road in central Nepal and plunged down a slope, Nepali police said. The bus was travelling on a road leading from a highway to a temple near Shahid Lakhan village, about 120 kilometres west of Kathmandu. The injured passengers were taken for medical treatment; authorities had not yet released further details about the cause of the crash.

What happened

On Saturday night, March 14, 2026, a bus carrying Indian pilgrims crashed while returning from Nepal’s Manakamana Temple, a popular Hindu shrine. The crash happened on a road leading from the highway toward the temple, near Shahid Lakhan village in central Nepal, about 120 kilometres west of Kathmandu. The bus was travelling downhill when it reportedly slipped at a curve, rolled down the mountainside for about 150 metres, and landed in a ravine. Seven Indian pilgrims died. Seven other Indian passengers were injured, as were the Nepali driver and his assistant. Police and villagers reached the wreckage, pulled out the injured, and helped transport them by ambulance to nearby hospitals. Police said autopsies would be carried out before the victims’ bodies were released to their families. The report did not give a confirmed cause beyond the bus slipping at the curve.
